# Original order

## Apply when

Apply when chapter order, message chronology, document structure, or existing captured content carries evidence. This principle is independently usable without sibling packages.

## Principle

Preserve the creator’s meaningful organization and sequence because relationships and workflow are evidence. Do not invent an original order where none is known.

## Knowledge-vault ingest

Keep chapter order, message chronology, headings, and captured source text verbatim. Separate compilation and annotations from original content. Existing Raw bodies remain unchanged: append evidence after the preserved body, and save improved or fuller extraction as a new linked Raw rather than silently replacing the earlier capture. Verify preserved bytes against the reviewed preimage, not guessed heading boundaries. Record conversion limitations separately in ingest notes.

Example: a three-message public discussion retains its sequence and attributions. A Concept note may synthesize it without rewriting or reordering the Raw transcript.

Read the destination’s live policy and exact task authorization. This contextual principle never authorizes a write, move, or deletion.
