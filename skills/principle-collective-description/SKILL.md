# Collective description

## Apply when

Apply when individual records cannot explain the scope, structure, or history of their collection alone. This principle is independently usable without sibling packages.

## Principle

Describe records at aggregate levels as well as individually so the structure, context, content, and management history of the whole remain intelligible.

## Knowledge-vault ingest

Describe the collection’s creator, scope, constituent structure, coverage, gaps, and acquisition or transformation history at the appropriate aggregate note. Link individual Raw captures rather than copying their original text into the description. A Paper hub explains available captures and their limitations; a Book Index supplies the source TOC and progress context. Do not imply complete coverage from one excerpt or repeat aggregate facts in every item.

Example: an example report hub lists an abstract capture and a later full-text capture separately, explains their coverage, and links both while leaving each original intact.

Read the destination’s live placement policy and exact task authorization. This contextual principle never authorizes a write, move, or deletion.
