# Conversations

Identify the selected conversation and its turn range before extraction. Keep speaker labels, timestamps and original turn order when they exist. Do not invent missing labels or infer deleted messages. Record inaccessible, collapsed or truncated turns in fidelity omissions.

An existing public locator is provenance, not permission to create a new share link. Never create a share link, upload the conversation or send it to a service as a capture step. Browser availability and successful extraction must be observed separately.

Use transcript when the requested range was obtained, excerpt for explicitly chosen excerpts, manifest-only when only the conversation locator or message list is available, and mixed for separately obtained members of a selected batch. A summary is Agent Capture Notes, not Original Content.

Read the destination's actual conversation/capture template when one is selected. Preserve its declared engine and required metadata; do not substitute an operational ingest receipt or work report for the conversation transcript. If its fields cannot express fidelity, report that integration gap rather than altering the template.
