# Mail

Read only the designated message or thread.
Use the actual message/thread identity and preserve sender/recipient roles, dates, quoted history and attachment references where authorized.
Keep private identifiers out of public source fields; an account or workstation path is not a public locator.
No mail arrival, label or scheduled collector implicitly authorizes compilation.

Retain the observed original/export and record inaccessible attachments, omitted replies and clipped content.
Do not merge distinct messages by subject or infer missing dates or participants.
Analysis cites the exact message and quote location rather than treating a summary as a new source.

Sending, forwarding, labeling, deleting and modifying mail are separate effects.
The helper can preserve a supplied UTF-8 export; it does not authenticate or query a mailbox.
