# Hierarchical management

## Apply when

Apply when records need collection-to-item context or ingest outputs need role-aware placement. This principle is independently usable without sibling packages.

## Principle

Arrange and describe records from the broadest contextual group to specific constituent records, with explicit relationships between levels. Understand an item through its parent context.

## Knowledge-vault ingest

Read the destination’s live placement policy before assigning paths. Distinguish Raw evidence from compiled Entity, Concept, Guide, and analysis notes. Link a chapter to its Book Index and a capture to its aggregate hub so readers can recover the larger context. Use the creator’s actual hierarchy when known. A vault’s storage hierarchy is an adaptation, not proof of historical original order. Do not create folders or move unrelated notes without authorization.

Example: an example book has a Book Index linked to its ordered chapter captures. Its author Entity is compiled knowledge, not a substitute for the original text or its hierarchy.

Read exact task authorization. This contextual principle never authorizes a write, move, or deletion.
